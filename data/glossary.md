# Health insurance glossary (plain English)

- **ICHRA**: Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangement. Your employer gives you a monthly amount; you buy your own individual plan and the employer reimburses you up to that amount.
- **Allowance**: The monthly amount your employer provides under the ICHRA.
- **Premium**: What you pay each month to keep the plan active, whether or not you use care.
- **Deductible**: What you pay yourself for covered care each year before the plan starts sharing costs.
- **Coinsurance**: Your percentage share of a bill after you meet the deductible. 30% coinsurance means you pay 30%, the plan pays 70%.
- **Copay**: A flat fee for a service, such as a doctor visit.
- **Out-of-pocket maximum**: The most you can pay in a year for covered care. After that the plan pays 100% of covered costs.
- **Formulary**: The list of prescription drugs a plan covers, grouped into tiers.
- **Prior authorization**: Approval the plan requires before covering certain drugs or procedures.
- **In-network**: Doctors and hospitals that have agreed prices with the plan. Out-of-network care usually costs more.
- **Bronze / Silver / Gold**: Plan levels. Bronze has lower premiums but higher costs when you get care. Gold has higher premiums but lower costs when you get care.
